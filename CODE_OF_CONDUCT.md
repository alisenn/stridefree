# Code of Conduct

StrideFree is a small, friendly open-source project. Everyone is welcome here — whatever your experience, background, language or the treadmill you own.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are first-time contributors or are writing in their second language.
- **Assume good faith.** Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for StrideFree.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, discriminatory jokes, sexual content, doxxing or unwelcome attention — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, email **canykimy@gmail.com**. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. You'll get a reply, and the maintainer may edit or remove content, or block people who break this code.
